Boards and rooms

Boards are numbered in order across the rooms. Every game gets a board, and you can move a game to another board from its card.

Fixed boards

Keep a team on the same board for every game, for example a Senior team that should stay put. The other teams rotate onto that board with them. Boards 1 and 3 are good spots for Senior teams.

Elimination stage

Work from the bottom up. Choose how many of the lowest-ranked teams play the Enosh Cup, then how many play the Resurrection. Everyone above them gets a bye straight to the semifinals. Only the winner of each game moves on.

2

Round robin

Pick who finished 1st, 2nd and so on in each game. Points are added to the standings as each game is scored.

3

Standings

Ranked by points, then by the number of 1st place finishes, then 2nd and so on. When teams tied on everything straddle a cutoff (byes, Resurrection or Enosh Cup), a team whose category, or whose brothers or sisters side, has no team yet in the higher group goes first. You can still move tied teams with the arrows, and your order then replaces the automatic one. Row colors show which group each team goes to.
